Basis: measured.

106 findings sit in 103 finding units. Mean words per narrative: 96.5 routed, 100.9 narrative-only.

## Rates

Measured: 2 sitting(s), 2.58 hours in all. The D-G model gives 2.69 hours (low) to 6.26 hours (high) for this packet, so the measured time is 0.96 times the low model and 0.41 times the high model.

Blended: 0.87 minutes per unit over every finding, narrative and audit unit, case facts included.

| Minutes per unit | Low split | High split |
|---|---:|---:|
| Finding unit (verdict) | 0.48 | 0.62 |
| Narrative unit, routed (whole read) | 1.01 | 0.87 |
| Narrative unit, narrative-only (D-J) | 1.05 | 0.90 |
| Audit unit | 0.48 | 0.82 |
| Case facts, per case | 1.44 | 1.24 |

The measured total is shared out by the D-G weights; the two splits add up to the same measured time. Per-kind minutes are apportioned, not timed one by one.

Fit by date not possible: 1 dated sittings for 3 unit kinds; a fit needs more dates than kinds.

Beside it (D-J): the narrative-only read of about 306 narratives per full run, 4.6 to 5.3 hours, against the stated 5 to 11.

Assumptions: each stratum scaled from its dev cases to 146 standard and 54 hard evaluation cases; route volume 1.00 for every worker (low) or 1.00, 1.05, 1.25 and 1.25 for Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5 and Opus 5.5 (high); case facts once per case for all four workers (low) or per worker (high); audit rows as scaled (low) or at least 70, 200, 70 and 70 (high); second look max(100, 10 percent) routed narrative units per worker run, each with its findings; narrative-only reads not scaled by route volume. Not included: transcript reading, breaks, the review tool's code work.
